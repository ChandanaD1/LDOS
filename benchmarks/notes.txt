Case 1 shows THP’s benefit (fewer TLB misses, fewer faults). 
Case 2 asks when that benefit becomes a cost. THP costs you in three ways:
- It needs a contiguous, aligned 2MB block, which can trigger compaction and reclaim stalls.
- Reclaim and swap work in 2MB units, so one hot 4KB piece keeps 2MB resident, or the page must be split.
- Swap-out of a whole THP needs a contiguous swap cluster. If there isn’t one, it splits, and swap-in then brings back 
4KB pages, which fragments swap further (your spiral).
